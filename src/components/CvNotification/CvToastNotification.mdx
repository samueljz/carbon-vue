## Overview

The `CvToastNotification` component cannot contain interactive elements or rich text.
These are announced by screenreaders when rendered. They don't grab focus.
Use them to provide the user with an alert, status, or log.

Toast notifications are typically used for brief, auto-dismissible messages and
support the `timeout` prop to automatically close after a specified duration.
